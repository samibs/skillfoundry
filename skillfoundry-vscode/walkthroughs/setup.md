## Connect your AI provider

SkillFoundry runs your build pipeline through an AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Google, or a local model).

You add a key **once**. It's stored in VS Code's encrypted **SecretStorage** — never written to disk in plaintext, never committed.

Pick a default provider now; you can change it later in settings. Everything else on this page uses that one choice — no per-action provider prompts.
