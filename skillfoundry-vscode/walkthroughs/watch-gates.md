## Watch the gates run

Open the **SkillFoundry** view in the Activity Bar (the shield icon) and watch the **Forge Monitor**.

The pipeline moves through six phases:

`IGNITE → FORGE → TEMPER → INSPECT → SPECTER → DEBRIEF`

As it runs, the Anvil quality gates light up pass/fail in real time. This is SkillFoundry's differentiator — not a wall of configuration to learn up front, but a checklist that proves rigor by *behavior*.

You never had to understand the gate taxonomy to benefit from it. That's the point.
